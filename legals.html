TERMS OF SERVICE

1.1 SCOPE OF SERVICES: Norden Global Handelhus AS and Salsabil International operate international freight procurement, customs brokerage, and logistics verification services subject to the General Conditions of the Nordic Association of Freight Forwarders (NSAB 2015).

1.2 SUPPLY CHAIN INTEGRITY: All suppliers onboarded onto the Salsabil International procurement grid undergo automated sanctions screening, ESG compliance verification, and certificate-of-origin validation.

1.3 LIMITATION OF LIABILITY: Freight transit liability is governed by mandatory maritime, road (CMR), and air transport conventions (Montreal Convention). Demurrage and port customs delays outside direct operational control are managed via transparent dispute resolution protocols.

PRIVACY POLICY & DATA SOVEREIGNTY

2.1 COMPLIANCE: We adhere strictly to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Norwegian Personal Data Act (Personopplysningsloven).

2.2 TELEMETRY & TELEMETRY RETENTION: Cargo sensor readings, vessel GPS coordinates, and temperature telemetry logs are stored in secure sovereign Nordic data vaults with cryptographic access auditing.

2.3 THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE: Manifest data is transmitted exclusively to authorized customs agencies (Norwegian Customs Tolletaten, EU Customs, UAE Customs) strictly as required for lawful import clearance.
